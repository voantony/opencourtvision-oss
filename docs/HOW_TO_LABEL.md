# How to label a point

A is always the near side of the court in that clip (closer to the camera). B is always the far side. The legend does not flip when the teams change ends, and shirt colour is not the guide.

`a_side_team` on a clip says which match team is on the near side (`A` or `B`). A call of A or B is the near or far side. Scoring and the human baseline translate that call back to the match team. Fault serve stays F. Clips with no `a_side_team` stay out of rounds until the ends are marked.

The warm-up clips `2p6PcKP0720_p4`, `2p6PcKP0720_p3`, and `2p6PcKP0720_p5` are public CC BY points used only to teach the three calls. They are not part of a round, not scored, and not part of the human baseline.

On 2p6PcKP0720 the near side is match team A for p1–p5 (same ends; p1 ends 13:37.6, p2 starts 13:49.6). On 632k3fKraeA p1–p14, team A is near in every clip. On GKWNVYeEAlE p1–p27, team A is near except p11 and p12, where A is far. The three warm-up clips stay out of rounds, so 43 clips are playable.
