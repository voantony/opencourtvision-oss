# References — Vamos Highlight v1

Public examples only. **Do not download or redistribute** their footage. Use for pacing, typography, grade, overlay density, and music behaviour study.

---

## What we take / what we reject

| Source class | Take | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Wivorn-class fan creators | Emotional beat choice, music that supports hits, clean title cards, restraint in *which* moments make the cut | Retiming action to music, mid-rally tease cuts, emoji/hype spam, face-centric hero zooms |
| ATP / Tennis TV / Grand Slam packages | Score-bug clarity, server-first reading, previous-set games on bug, high contrast chalk-on-night, hard cuts between points | Tournament-specific purple/green chrome (we keep Vamos Volt), dense mid-rally stats popups |
| Broadcast score bugs (Wimbledon 2025, US Open SMT) | Fixed name rows, seeds/serve mark, expandable stats *pattern* (we stay compact) | Full AR / drone graphics, face recognition lower-thirds |
