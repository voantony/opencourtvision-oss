## Help make Vamos good
Vamos is a free, open-source match tracker for tennis and padel. It runs on your computer and your footage stays there. We're looking for a small group of testers to try it and tell us what breaks.

**You don't need to give your name or email.** We'll reply here on this issue.

### About your setup
- **Sport:** tennis / padel / both
- **Computer:** model and chip (e.g. MacBook Air M2)
- **How you film:** phone on the fence / tripod / other (say what)
- **Level (optional):** e.g. NTRP 3.5, club 4th division, "just for fun"
- **Language:** English / Español

### Before you join
- [ ] I'm 18 or older.
- [ ] Every player in footage I use with Vamos agreed to be filmed, and no one on court is under 18.

### What happens next
We'll reply here with how to run the project and our short guide on how to film a match for Vamos ([docs/filming.md](../../docs/filming.md)). To leave or delete anything you've sent us, open a confidential issue with the delete-request template. Deleting data on your computer is local to you.
