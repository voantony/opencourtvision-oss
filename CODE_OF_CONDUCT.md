# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Critique the change, not the person. No harassment, and no private match footage, faces, or contact details in issues, pull requests, or commits.

If something happened that you cannot raise on the issue tracker, email antonyvo@mail.grokbot.com. Reports are confidential. Maintainers may edit or reject material that breaks this, including anything that publishes someone else's match.

This is the project's own short standard. It is not the Contributor Covenant.
