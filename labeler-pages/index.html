Label padel points

OpenCourtVision volunteer labeler, session-01: 46 short clips from public CC BY padel matches. Watch each point and pick who won it and who served.

How it works

  • A is the team on the near side (closest to the camera) in the first clip of each match. Teams swap ends during a match, so follow the shirts.
  • S means skip or unclear: you can't tell who won, it isn't a real point, or the clip only shows a fault serve.
  • Server is the team that served the point. Leave it as "not sure" if you can't tell.
  • Keys: A B S for the winner, 1 server A, 2 server B, 0 server not sure, R replay, ← → previous / next.

Use letters, numbers, dots, dashes or underscores only.

Send your answers

The issue button opens a new confidential issue on gitlab.com/antonyvo/opencourtvision with your answers filled in. Sign in to GitLab, check it, and press "Create issue". Nothing is sent until you do. You can also attach labels.json to an issue yourself.

Privacy

This page collects only your GitLab username and your answers. They stay in this browser (local storage) until you download or send them. There are no cookies, no analytics, and no trackers, and the page loads nothing from other sites.

To delete labels you have sent, open a confidential delete-my-data issue or email antonyvo@mail.grokbot.com.
